How AI Search Changes Visibility

AI systems like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews do not hand out a list of links. They assemble an answer from the sources they trust, so the brands they understand best get named.

🔍

Traditional SEO

Optimises a single page to rank in a retrieved list of links. Relies on backlinks and keyword relevance. On its own, it doesn't explain which brands an AI system chooses to name.

🧠

AI Search

Reconstructs an answer from corroborated information across many platforms, and names the brands it can verify and trust.

🎯

The Result

When someone asks ChatGPT "who should I use for [your service] in Australia?", the brands with clear, consistent, verified signals are the ones that get named.

The disciplines behind this are Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO) and Answer Engine Optimisation (AEO), explained in full by Reviewly.
