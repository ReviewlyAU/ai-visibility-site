State of AI Visibility in Australia 2026

Published July 2026 By Reviewly Updated November 2026

Australians are asking AI tools such as ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews for recommendations, and many Australian businesses still don't appear in those answers for their own service category. This report sets out what is changing, why it matters for service businesses, and where to start.

What Is Changing

Published surveys from Roy Morgan Research, the Australian Bureau of Statistics and industry publications all point the same way: a growing share of Australians use AI tools every month, and more buyers start a search by asking an AI tool rather than scanning a list of links.

An AI answer works differently from a results page. Instead of ten links, the buyer gets a short answer that names a few businesses. If a business isn't named, it may never be considered.

Specific figures will be added to this page once each one has been checked against its original publication.

Why Many Businesses Are Missing

Most businesses were built for traditional search: a website, a Google Business Profile and some directory listings. AI systems look for something more. They want to confirm who a business is, what it does and why it's worth choosing, using independent sources that agree with each other.

When a business's name, services or locations differ from one platform to the next, or when it has little independent evidence beyond its own website, AI systems have less to go on and tend to name someone else.

The Business Size Divide

Larger Australian businesses have generally adopted AI tools faster than small and micro businesses, according to ABS business surveys. Larger organisations are more likely to be investing in clear, structured information about themselves, while many smaller businesses haven't yet looked at how they appear in AI answers.

For small and medium businesses, that is a reason to check now rather than later. The work is mostly about accuracy and consistency, which suits businesses that know their customers well.

What AI Visibility Means for Service Businesses

When a buyer asks an AI tool to recommend a plumber, accountant or funeral home in their area, the answer names a small number of businesses. Being one of them puts a business in front of the buyer at the moment they are deciding.

No one can guarantee that an AI system will name a particular business. What a business can do is make sure the information about it is accurate, consistent, verified and easy for AI systems to understand. Reviewly's REVIEW Method™ describes the five signals involved: Recognised, Established, Verified, Influential and Enduring.

Where to Start

  • Ask ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google which businesses they would recommend for your service in your area, and note whether you are named.
  • Check that your business name, address, phone number and services match everywhere you are listed.
  • Look at where independent sources mention you: reviews, industry bodies, local media and directories.
  • Make sure each key page on your website answers its main question clearly near the top.
